CEO Simulator (LLM2WMD): Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no servers of its own. We do not collect, send, share or sell any personal data.

What is stored on your device

Your progress, statistics and settings (sound, reduce flashing) are saved on your own device, in the app's or browser's local storage. They never leave your device unless you choose to copy your save code somewhere. Clearing the app's or browser's data deletes them.

Children

The game is a satire for teenagers and adults, with dark jokes. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Satire notice

The game is parody. Any resemblance to real people is the audience's own conclusion. Numbers and effects are game mechanics, not claims of fact.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/chrisllm2wmd-prog/the-collective. (A support email will be added before the store release.)
